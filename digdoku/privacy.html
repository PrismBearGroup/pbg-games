Digdoku: Treasure Race: privacy policy

Prism Bear Group Ltd (Berkhamsted, United Kingdom) makes Digdoku: Treasure Race. This policy covers the game on the web and the Android app on Google Play. Last updated 11 November 2026.

What the game records

The game sends anonymous play statistics to a server we run (Cloudflare Workers and D1) so we can tune it: when the tutorial, a round, a level or the day's map starts and ends, how long it took, how many spades were lost, which items were used, the build version, the platform and whether the screen is a touch screen. They are added into daily counts. There is no account, no name, no email and no identifier of any kind in these records: nothing links one play to the next, or to you. Your IP address carries the request and is not stored.

Your progress (levels, gold, relics, settings) is kept on your device: in the browser's storage on the web, in the app's own storage on Android. On a games portal such as Playgama, the portal keeps the save and runs its own software under its own privacy policy.

Adverts in the Android app

The Android app shows adverts served by Google AdMob: one when you leave a finished map, and, only when you choose them, rewarded adverts that double a map's gold or pay for an item. Adverts are capped at Google's "PG" content rating. To serve, personalise, limit and measure adverts and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address and the approximate location worked out from it, information about the device, how you interact with the adverts, and diagnostic data such as how long the app took to start. Google describes this in how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Where the law requires your consent (the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown before any advert, and adverts are personalised only if you agree. The "Advert privacy choices" button in the game's Settings, under Privacy, opens the form again so you can change or withdraw consent; where Google needs no consent the button is not shown.

What we do not do

No cookies of our own, no tracking across apps or sites, no selling of data, and no data knowingly collected from children: the game is made for players aged 13 and over. The app asks for no permission beyond the network.

Your choices

Delete the app or clear the site's data to remove your progress. Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Because our own statistics hold no identifier, there is nothing of yours for us to look up or delete. Questions: admin@prismbeargroup.com.

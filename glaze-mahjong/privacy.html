Glaze Mahjong: privacy policy

Prism Bear Group Ltd (Berkhamsted, United Kingdom) makes Glaze Mahjong. This policy covers the game on the web and the Android app on Google Play. Last updated 26 November 2026.

What the game records

The game sends anonymous play statistics to a server we run (Cloudflare Workers and D1) so we can tune it: when a board or the day's board starts and ends, how long it took, how many stars it earned, which helpers were used (hint, undo, shuffle), what gold was spent on, whether an advert was shown, the build version, the platform and whether the screen is a touch screen. They are added into daily counts. There is no account, no name, no email and no identifier of any kind in these records: nothing links one play to the next, or to you. Your IP address carries the request and is not stored.

Your progress (boards, gold, your wall, settings) is kept on your device: in the browser's storage on the web, in the app's own storage on Android. On a games portal such as Playgama or CrazyGames, the portal keeps the save and runs its own software under its own privacy policy.

Adverts in the Android app

The Android app shows adverts served by Google AdMob. There is none in your first ten minutes of play in a visit. After that one may show between boards, at most one every two and a half minutes, and, only when you choose it, a rewarded advert that pays for hints or a free shuffle. Adverts are capped at Google's "PG" content rating. To serve, personalise, limit and measure adverts and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address and the approximate location worked out from it, information about the device, how you interact with the adverts, and diagnostic data such as how long the app took to start. Google describes this in how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Where the law requires your consent (the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown before any advert, and adverts are personalised only if you agree. The "Advert privacy choices" button at the foot of the game's wall screen opens the form again so you can change or withdraw consent; where Google needs no consent the button is not shown.

What we do not do

No cookies of our own, no tracking across apps or sites, no selling of data, and no data knowingly collected from children: the game is made for players aged 13 and over. The app asks for no permission beyond the network.

Your choices

Delete the app or clear the site's data to remove your progress. Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Because our own statistics hold no identifier, there is nothing of yours for us to look up or delete. Questions: admin@prismbeargroup.com.
